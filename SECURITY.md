# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability
Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

1. Use "Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab of the repository (GitHub private vulnerability reporting), or
2. Email hello@haorgrix.com with the subject "Security: <repository name>".

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the version or commit. Do not include real customer data.

## What to expect
- We confirm receipt within 3 working days.
- We tell you whether we accept the report, and the expected fix time, within 10 working days.
- We credit you in the release notes if you want that.

## Scope
Code in repositories of the HaorGrix organization. Client systems, third-party services and social engineering are out of scope.

## Safe harbour
If you act in good faith, test only against your own installs, and give us reasonable time to fix, we will not take legal action against you.
